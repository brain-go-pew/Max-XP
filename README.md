## Project Purpose

This repository is the team’s shared workspace for the Stage 1 GitHub and Markdown assignment. Its purpose is to develop the first substantial version of the **AI Overview** module’s learning content in Markdown.

The Markdown content in this repository is intended to become the shared foundation for the module’s:

- Study Guide
- PPT Slides
- Video Tutorials

Version 1.0 will contain substantive learning material—not only headings or a future plan. The developed content will include accurate beginner-friendly explanations, definitions of unfamiliar terms, at least one worked example or demonstration, a useful visual, a learner activity or application question, an explained answer or expected outcome, and identifiable references.

This README identifies the team, the module, the intended learners, and the planned locations of the project materials. The content `.md` files will be created and updated in the repository by team members.

## Intended Learners

This study guide is designed for **university students and general internet users** who want to understand how Google AI Overviews work, how to use them effectively, and how to evaluate the reliability of AI-generated answers.

It is especially suitable for **beginners with limited knowledge of AI** who want to develop their AI literacy, critical thinking, and information verification skills.

Typical intended learners may include:

- Students encountering AI concepts for the first time
- General learners who want a clear, non-technical overview of AI
- Learners who need a foundation before studying more advanced AI or machine-learning topics

By the end of the module, beginners should be able to:

1. Understand AI Overviews and explain what they are and why they exist.
2. Explain the fundamentals of AI and how AI Overviews generate answers.
3. Describe the AI search process, from user queries to AI-generated summaries and citations.
4. Use AI Overviews effectively by developing better queries and verifying information.
5. Evaluate answer quality based on accuracy, relevance, completeness, and citation reliability.
6. Identify limitations and failure modes, including hallucinations, outdated information, and misleading answers.
7. Assess user experience by considering usability, trust, accessibility, and inclusivity.
8. Recognize risks and responsibilities, including bias, misinformation, privacy, and publisher impact.
9. Understand the business impact of AI Overviews on search engines, SEO, and online content.
10. Apply the knowledge gained to critically evaluate AI Overviews and make informed decisions about when to trust their answers.

**Assumed prerequisite knowledge:** basic computer literacy and the ability to read English. No programming experience is required unless a specific section states otherwise. Any additional prerequisites should be stated at the start of the relevant section.

## Review and Coordination

A team member other than the author will review the developed material. The review will check:

- Whether definitions, claims, examples, and answers are correct
- Whether beginners can follow the explanation without unexplained prerequisites
- Whether activities can be completed using the information provided
- Whether sources exist and support the relevant claims
- Whether images, file references, and supporting assets work in the submitted copy

At least one concrete review outcome will be recorded in `review-log.md`: what was checked, what was found, and what was corrected or confirmed.
